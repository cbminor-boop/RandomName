# Topic

Economic outlook: how U.S. imports compare year over year.

# Questions

1. What was the total value of U.S. imports (goods and services) in the most
   recent reported period, and how does it compare with the same period a year
   earlier, in dollars and in percent?
2. How did goods imports and services imports each change year over year?
3. For the year to date, how do total imports compare with the same months of
   the previous year?
4. Which major categories of goods imports (for example, consumer goods,
   capital goods, industrial supplies, autos) rose or fell the most compared
   with a year earlier?
5. How did imports from the largest trading partners (for example, China,
   Mexico, Canada, the European Union) change compared with a year earlier?
6. How did the trade deficit change year over year, and how much of that change
   came from imports?
7. What does the source say about the outlook for imports in the coming months
   or year, and what reasons does it give?
